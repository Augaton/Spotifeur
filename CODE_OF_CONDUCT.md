# Code of Conduct

Spotifeur is a small open-source project. Keep discussions useful, respectful and focused on the app and its documentation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith, and keep technical claims specific and verifiable.
- Stay on topic in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Report bugs with enough context for someone else to reproduce them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Sharing private information without permission, including account names, tokens, logs with credentials or other people's data.
- Posting Spotify binaries, decompiled Spotify code, keys, cracked accounts or links to them.
- Posting malicious files, phishing links or unsafe installation instructions.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues and block users who make the project unsafe or unproductive.

To report a conduct issue, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub, or open a minimal issue asking for a private channel.
